lesson: 5.1 - Homeostasis
MC | 1 | What is homeostasis? | The regulation of conditions inside the body to keep them constant | The growth and repair of the cells and tissues that make up the body | The breakdown of large food molecules into smaller ones that can be absorbed | The movement of blood around the body by the heart through arteries and veins | Homeostasis keeps conditions such as temperature and blood glucose within narrow limits.

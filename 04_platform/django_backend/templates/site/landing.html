How it works

Pick a topic

Every GCSE topic is split into short lessons, ordered like the AQA specification, so you can revise exactly what is coming up.

Practise it

Questions after every lesson. Get one wrong and you see why, not just the answer, with a hint for the next try.

Come back to mistakes

Missed questions and tricky flashcards return on a schedule, so you remember them in the exam, not just tonight.

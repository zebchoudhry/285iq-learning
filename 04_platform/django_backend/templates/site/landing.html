How it works

📅

1. Tell us your exam month

Pick your subjects and when your exams are. That sets your readiness target and how much time you have.

⚡

2. Do your 10 minutes

One button picks today's session for you. No deciding what to revise. You see why each answer is right, with a hint for the next try.

🔁

3. Watch weak spots shrink

Topics you struggle with get more of your time until you answer them well. Then they drop to a quick refresher.

Everything for revision, in one place

🧠

Practice that sticks

Testing yourself beats rereading notes. Every lesson ends in questions, and wrong answers are asked again.

🔢

Fresh numbers every time

Calculation questions in maths, physics and chemistry change their numbers, so you learn the method, not the answer.

🃏

Flashcards on a schedule

Spaced-repetition flashcards show you each card just before you would forget it.

🎯

A mistake bank

Every question you miss is saved, with the mistake type, so you can see what to fix first.

⚔️

Boss battles and games

Six quick games and a boss battle for every topic, with XP, levels and combos to keep you going.

📝

Mock tests

Practise under timed conditions once you are getting close, and see what to fix before the real thing.
